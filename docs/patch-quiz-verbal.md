# Patch trip quiz — verbal and result map

## Working title

**Where Should Your Wild Side Go Next?**

Seven quick questions. One very Patchie answer.

## 30-second verbal

Not sure which adventure has your name on it? We made you a shortcut. Tell us how you like to travel—the pace, the stories, the food, the amount of wild you are willing to welcome—and in under two minutes we will match you with the Patch trip that feels most like you.

It is not a personality test with a passport glued on. It is a warm introduction to the place, people and kind of adventure you are most likely to love. Ready to meet your next trip?

## Result-page pattern

Lead with the traveller identity, then explain why it fits. Show one primary and one secondary trip, an honest activity-and-comfort note, and two actions: **See my trip** and **Talk it through with a Patchie**.

Do not gate the result. Offer email or SMS only after the result is visible. Dates, availability and prices must come from current trip data rather than quiz copy. The bookings team still confirms physical fit.
